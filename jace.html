DJ Jace

SMOKE DOUBT JACE

Smoke Doubt Jace is an Ottawa-born hip-hop artist and foundational voice within the independent collective 'theWildCore'. Jace built his reputation on gritty delivery, shock energy, and an uncompromising dedication to the craft. Started as a B-Boy, expanded to a prolific MC and Graff Writer. Today, based in the Dominican Republic, DJ Jace brings that same raw, substance-driven energy to the DJ booth.. Connecting his 613 roots with global crowds.
